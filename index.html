05 · WHAT WE HAVE DISCOVERED

Early evidence points to a complex picture.

01

Youth offending is not simply increasing.

Long-term NSW data indicates substantial declines in youth offending.

02

Reoffending remains concentrated.

A smaller but important group experiences particularly high rates of subsequent offending.

03

Intervention does not automatically equal effectiveness.

Evaluation of Youth on Track found mixed outcomes, including no statistically significant overall effect on reoffending and custody outcomes.

04

Community relationships may matter.

Mentoring evidence highlights matching, supervision, engagement and community networks.

Sources: BOCSAR (2022; 2026); NSW Department of Communities and Justice (2022).
