1. Criminological FocusSlide 3 / 16

Our Specific Research Focus

Moving away from generic youth justice debates to address a modern vector

Rather than evaluating juvenile correctional facilities broadly, our group focus narrows down tightly to investigate Digital Recidivism Pathways among juveniles aged 14-18 post-release.

Why Digital Space?

Traditional criminological models map criminal peer contact using geographical neighborhoods. Modern juvenile offenders utilize encrypted, unmonitored digital networks, making physical monitoring inadequate.

📚 Smith, A. & Jones, B. (2024). Digital spaces and juvenile recidivism. *Journal of Criminology*, 45(2), 112-125.
