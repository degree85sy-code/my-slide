4. The Identified Institutional Gap

Through text-mining and policy analysis of the partner organisation's guidelines, we mapped out a structural vulnerability:

The "Digital Transition Blindspot": Excellent physical rehabilitation exists inside the facility, but a complete data vacuum occurs post-release.

Vulnerabilities of the Current Gap

  • Reintegration Friction: High psychological shock during community re-entry.
  • Peer Pull: Digital algorithms active on minor devices actively push toxic peer notifications.
  • Resource Misallocation: Budget spent on physical tracking fails to stop virtual criminal planning.
